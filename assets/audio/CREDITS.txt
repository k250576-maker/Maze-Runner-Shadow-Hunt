Deep Dungeon — Fog Saburo
Optional main-menu soundtrack. The supplied recording runs 3:02. The supplied

Footsteps (footsteps-use-it-again.mp3)
Preferred local footstep source. The game crops one step in memory. The creator and redistribution license are
unverified. Keep the supplied recording out of distributed builds.

Stone step (stone_step.wav)
Original synthesized sound made for this project. The game uses this fallback clip,
with a short envelope applied in memory.

Player damage impact
Original descending pulse and noise transient synthesized in memory for this game.

Enemy effects
Original procedural stone, whisper, metal, rasp, sword, lunge, and impact
samples synthesized in memory for this game. The Ghost strike and soul chime
are original procedural sounds as well.
